FAQ

Common questions

How many players can play at once? ▼
Letter Frenzy supports 2 to 8 players in a single room. It works best with 3–6, but the more the merrier — more players means more chances for creative clashes and funny challenges.
Do I need to download anything? ▼
No download, no app store, no account. Just open play.letterfrenzy.co.uk on any device with a browser and you're in. Everyone plays from their own phone or tablet — no shared screen needed.
Is it free? ▼
Yes, completely free — no ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases. Just show up and play.
Can we use our own categories? ▼
Absolutely. The host can add, remove, or edit any of the default categories when setting up a game. You can go with the classics or get creative — Movies That Make You Cry, Things in a Junk Drawer, whatever works for your group.
How long does a game take? ▼
A typical 3-round game with 60-second timers takes around 15–25 minutes. You can adjust the number of rounds and timer length when setting up, so you can fit a quick game into a break or go longer for a full game night.
What devices does it work on? ▼
Any device with a modern browser — iPhone, Android, tablet, laptop, desktop. No specific browser required. As long as everyone has a connection and a browser, they're good to go.
Can I play remotely with friends in different locations? ▼
Yes! Letter Frenzy works over any internet connection — great for family game nights over video call. Share the room code and everyone can join from wherever they are.
Who is the host and what can they do? ▼
The person who creates the room is the host. They choose the categories, rounds, and timer length, and start the game when everyone's ready. During the review phase, the host can override any challenge vote if there's a dispute.
What are the answer hints shown during review? ▼
For five popular categories — Country, Animal, Fruit, Colour, and Sport — the review screen shows a small hint next to each answer: ✓ Recognised means the answer is in our curated list; ⚠️ Not recognised means it isn't. These are guides only — players can still challenge anything, and the group vote plus host override always have the final say.

How to Play
🎯

The Goal

Fill every category with a word starting with the round's letter — faster and more uniquely than your opponents. Unique answers score more, so creativity beats speed.

How a round works
1

A letter is revealed

A random letter is drawn from the host's chosen difficulty pool — 🟢 Easy, ⚡ Standard, or 🔥 Hard — or players secretly nominate a letter and one is chosen from the pool, keeping everyone guessing.

2

Race to fill the categories

Type an answer for every category — all must start with the round's letter. The clock is ticking. Hit Submit early to lock in your answers.

3

Review, challenge, and score

All answers are revealed. Flag anything that seems wrong and the group votes. Points are awarded automatically once the round is settled.

Scoring
10 pts
A valid answer nobody else wrote — unique answers are rewarded
5 pts
A valid answer but someone else wrote something very similar
0 pts
Blank, wrong starting letter, or voted invalid by the group
⚠️

Challenges & Voting

Spot a dodgy answer on the review screen? Hit Flag to challenge it. Everyone except the answer's owner gets 30 seconds to vote 👍 valid or 👎 invalid. The majority wins — the host breaks ties and can override any decision at any time.

💡 Tips for winning

Submit early. Locking your answers before time's up lets you see how many others have submitted — and saves you from last-minute panic.

Go obscure. Weird-but-valid answers score just as much as obvious ones. If nobody else writes the same thing, you get full points.

In Pick mode, be sneaky. Your letter nomination is secret — pick one that's easy for you and hard for the rest of the room.

Hosts set the difficulty. Choose 🟢 Easy for a family-friendly letter pool, ⚡ Standard for the full range minus the really tricky ones, or 🔥 Hard to unlock Q, X, Z and everything in between.

🔍

Answer Hints

For five popular categories — Country, Animal, Fruit, Colour, and Sport — the review screen shows a small hint next to each answer. A ✓ Recognised tick means the answer matched our curated list; ⚠️ Not recognised means it didn't. These hints guide discussion — they're not final rulings. The group vote and host override always have the last say.
