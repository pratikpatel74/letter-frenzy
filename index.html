How to Play
🎯 The Goal
Fill every category with a word starting with the round's letter — faster and more uniquely than your opponents.
How a Round Works
1
A letter is revealed
Random draw, or all players secretly nominate a letter and one is chosen from the pool.
2
Race to fill the categories
Type answers for every category — all must start with the round's letter. The timer counts down.
3
Scoring
Answers are scored automatically when time's up or everyone submits.
Scoring
10pt
Valid answer that nobody else gave
5pt
Valid answer but someone else wrote something very similar
0pt
Blank, wrong starting letter, or ruled invalid
⚠️ Challenges & Voting
Spot a dodgy answer? Tap Flag on the review screen. Everyone (except the answer's owner) gets 30 seconds to vote 👍 valid or 👎 invalid. Majority wins — the host breaks ties and can override any vote at any time.
💡 Tips
• Hit Submit early to lock in your answers — you'll see how many others have submitted.
• Obscure-but-valid answers score the same as common ones, as long as nobody else wrote the same thing.
• In Pick mode, your letter nomination is secret — try to pick one that's easy for you and hard for others!
